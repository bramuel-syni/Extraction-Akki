## §2 — The gap class this brief exists for: the commissioning envelope

Measured against the bundle (string-level, not impression): **milestone — 0 occurrences.
done-condition — 0. lawful basis — 0. demand evidence — 0. calibration — 1.**

The wizard journeys are strong on shape (need → reflection → estate intelligence → plan
bands → sample-before-commit → priced commit with hard ceiling) and then **commit on
price alone**. The canon's accountability structure between plan and spend is not
captured, and it is the load-bearing feature of the commissioning promise:

- **FB-4 Milestones before commit (all three doors).** The calibration/sample stage's
  output to the owner is **a proposed milestone list — the checklist of what must be done,
  in what order, for the commission to succeed — each milestone carrying a done-condition
  and an owner. The owner agrees the list at commission; the commission does not open
  until it is agreed; all reporting thereafter is against it.** (Audio Plane §11.3, §13.2;
  Quality Rule Book §33A: accountability set before spend is steerable; discovered during
  spend is only explicable.) Insert between the sample stage and the commit card. The
  commit CTA is disabled until the list is agreed.
- **FB-5 The Commission View.** The tracking surface for every commissioned work —
  extraction, training, export, integration alike; no modality gets its own interface.
  Front page is the agreed milestone checklist: each milestone, its done-condition, its
  owner, status in plain terms — done · on track · behind · **missed shown as plainly as
  done** — with spend against quote at the same plain level. Technical material
  (calibration results, coverage, checks) is drill-down evidence, never the front page.
  The existing trainingRun screen (stages, checkpoints, acceptance checks moving to
  settled) is kept **as the drill-down layer** beneath the milestone view, not as the view.
  Constraint carried from spec: milestones are records with done-conditions the backend
  already stamps — no element of this surface may require new backend computation beyond
  reading existing artifacts.
- **FB-6 The envelope completes.** A commission cannot freeze without: lawful basis,
  done-condition, budget (captured — the hard-ceiling language is right and stays),
  scope ceiling, and the availability snapshot. Lawful basis and done-condition are
  currently absent from every door. Admission refuses without lawful basis
  (missing_lawful_basis) — the wizard must therefore ask for it; it is a mandatory field,
  asked, never assumed.
- **FB-7 Grounding marker + sample cost.** The commit card states whether the objective
  is sample-grounded or estimate-grounded, and the sample's cost is shown drawing the
  objective's budget. Both are one-line additions to journeys that already run the sample.
- **FB-8 Commit review separation.** The 'assumed' field-state exists (good — the
  evidence floor renders as assumed in the export door). Complete it: the commit step
  separates *what you supplied* from *what was assumed — confirm or change*, per value,
  before freeze. The seam between user-said and agent-inferred stays visible to the
  moment of commitment.

## §3 — Journey completion audit (what the prototype already captures, and where each journey stops)

**CAPTURED WELL — keep, wire as-is:**
- Sample-before-commit on all three doors, with honest rows ("Held for review, not
  guessed"; "barely moved — stated, not hidden").
- Hard budget ceiling with halt-not-exceed language on every commit card.
- Cannot-cover stated before commitment, per door ("that data was never held").
- Rights-first ordering on the export door ("Rights come first here — before scope,
  before price"); non-exportable model lineage note on the train door.
- Rule change as visible cancelable pipeline (proposed → counter-signed → waiting →
  applied, cancel-before-applies).
- Deletion under dual control with waiting period and destruction attestation.
- Verification Runner ("Verify the Rules" — live packs, run-all, sign the go-live record).
- Quarantine with both dispositions rendered (released back · destroyed, run continues).
- Three-lens proof walk (Answer / Reasoning / Raw trail) as progressive tabs.
- Memo inheriting the answer's receipts rather than restating numbers (§38.1 action row,
  captured verbatim in spirit).
- Constitutional-seat succession screen (co-signer / sponsor) — beyond expectation.
- Integrate door: scoped key, named artifacts, test-call-before-live including a refusal
  and a no-evidence case, "the key cannot widen itself."

**INCOMPLETE — the journey exists and stops early:**
- **FB-9 Objective tracking** shows runs; coverage-to-objective (the % of the goal the
  holding now serves, with the basis of the figure) is not rendered. The commissioner
  watches coverage, not a progress bar.
- **FB-10 Gap filing.** Coverage-gap refusals do not file work. The canon's loop —
  unanswerable question → extraction candidate carrying demand evidence (how many
  distinct asks cited it) → self-ranking queue → commissioned against the top — is the
  demand-discovery flywheel and is absent end to end (0 hits beyond CSS `gap:`).
  One action on the refusal card files the gap; the Use Data module gains the ranked
  candidate list.
- **FB-11 Quote lifecycle tail.** Price and ceiling exist; validity window with visible
  expiry, delivery estimate as a range, and cancellation terms with itemised
  work-completed liability do not. These are what make acceptance a commercial record.
- **FB-12 Async delivery states.** accepted → running → delivered | refused appear in
  fragments; the developer journey renders the full contract including **late refusal as
  a governed terminal state** (accepted, then refused, is normal — never an error).
- **FB-13 Retention posture.** The unset-rule banner class ("the system holds everything
  indefinitely until you set a window — a decision only you can make") is not present.
  The Govern estate states retention honestly, including unset.
- **FB-14 Integration plane settings.** The door scopes read + rate + retention; missing
  at commissioning: write-back on/off with volume ceiling, storage ceiling, webhook
  registration, sandbox toggle, and the publication act (plane-local → Registry-visible,
  never automatic) as a later governed action in the developer surface.
- **FB-15 Opportunity briefs.** The seed-into-wizard prefill exists (kept — it is the
  correct only-exit). Missing per brief: the grounding line as live registry reads, the
  real-world precedent chip, the three-section shape (what exists / what it could
  become / what is missing), and the advisory marker on every row and brief — pending
  the FB-3(b) copy ruling for its exact wording.
- **FB-16 Waiting-period countdown.** The waiting period is stated; the visible countdown
  with cancel-until-applied is the spec'd rendering. Small; disproportionately load-bearing
  in a regulator demo.
