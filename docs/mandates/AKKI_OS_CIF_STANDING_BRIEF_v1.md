## §1 · STANDING DECLARATION

CIF is a **standalone engine** — the sixth, peer to Northena, Solva, Mtafiti,
Targeta, and SyniSense — supporting **ideation and the impact of consistently
increasing or reserved knowledge** (Owner's mandate statement, verbatim). It is
not a plane feature and not a subsystem of any other engine.

Its constitutional shape (spec §3, §9):
- It operates inside the engine walls everywhere: detection never decides;
  learning never widens what an objective may reach.
- The **coach seat** (origination port) is constitutionally load-bearing and
  permanent: new frames enter only through the coach, ground reality, or
  genuinely outside reviewers. Self-certification of frames is closed by
  construction (§9.1) — no parameter set can license coach-independence,
  because any evidence for it would be evaluated inside the frame it claims to
  transcend. The loop closes through the coach seat, never around it (§9.2).
- It is in force across the architecture on adoption — no phasing, no
  activation gates (§2, §14.1) — consistent with the single-experiment
  principle: nothing below system level is experimental.
- It is entry #1 in its own archive: manifested, rented, revisable under its
  own rules (§13.3). Its yield counts in owner-confirmed catches only;
  self-graded value is inadmissible (§13.1).
